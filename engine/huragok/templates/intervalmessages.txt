# Interval messages: said to everyone on every server, again and again, each one
# after its own time. A Discord link, the rules, anything you like.
#
#   how often | the message
#
# how often  30s, 10m, 1h, 1d, or put together: 1h30m. Once a minute at most.
# message    As players read it, from [Server]. A long one goes out as a few lines.
#
# Examples:
#
#   30m | HELLO, TRAVELLER. MOMBASA WELCOMES YOU! Discord: discord.gg/yourserver
#   1h  | KEEP IT CLEAN! No slurs, no cheating: Vergil is listening.
#   45m | BE A HERO! REPORT VIOLATORS! Tell a moderator what you saw.
#
# Huragok speaks to players as New Mombasa's city AI, the Superintendent, in the
# words it put on the city's signs and speakers in Halo 3: ODST: PLEASE REMAIN
# CALM! before a restart, CRIME DOESN'T PAY! when Vergil removes someone. Yours
# can too. Some of its words, as it said them:
#   KEEP IT CLEAN!                    OBEY POSTED LIMITS!
#   BE A HERO! REPORT VIOLATORS!      YOUR TAX DOLLARS AT WORK!
#   CRIME DOESN'T PAY!                GOOD CITIZENS DO THEIR PART!
#   PLEASE REMAIN CALM!               PARDON OUR DUST!
#   YIELD TO UPHILL TRAFFIC!          RECYCLING! DO YOUR PART!
#   WATCH YOUR SPEED! CHILDREN AT PLAY!
#   HELLO, TRAVELLER. MOMBASA WELCOMES YOU!
#   NO PARKING! VIOLATORS WILL BE TICKETED AND TOWED!
#   ANXIOUS? STRESSED? PLEASE REMAIN CALM.
#
# Messages with the same time take turns: two every 30m go out 15 minutes apart, each
# one every 30 minutes. They're only said while someone is on, counting from when
# Huragok first sees the server up.
# Each server folder has an intervalmessages.txt of its own, for messages that are
# only that server's; this one's are said on every server.
